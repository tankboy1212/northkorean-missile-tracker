Inspiration

This page was inspired by a video shared on X in 2026 that counted Japanese Ministry of Defense announcements of North Korean missile launches and reached a running total of "233+" by August 2026. What that post contributed is the idea: showing those launches as a map that moves through time. Everything on this page was built separately from it — the dataset, the launch-by-launch reconstruction, the EEZ verdicts and their linkage to individual MOD statements, the context timeline, the wording and all of the code. No frame, figure, graphic, text or data file from the original post is reproduced or re-used here, and the totals on this page deliberately differ from it because a different source is being counted. Original post: credit line and link to be added by the author.
